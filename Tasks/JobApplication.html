Hyundai Motor India (HMI)

Description

Company Overview: Hyundai Motor India Limited is a wholly-owned subsidiary of South Korea's Hyundai Motor Company. It stands as India's second-largest passenger car manufacturer and serves as a major export hub for global markets. The company focuses on building premium, smart, and eco-friendly vehicles integrated with high-end safety frameworks and connected ecosystem technologies.

Corporate Presence

  • Headquarters: Hyundai Motor India Limited, 1st Floor, Tower A, SDF Building, No. 1, Sector 16A, Film City Road, Noida - 201301, Uttar Pradesh, India.
  • Manufacturing Plants: Hyundai operates two state-of-the-art manufacturing plants in India: the Sriperumbudur Plant in Tamil Nadu and the Chakan Plant in Maharashtra.
  • Research & Development Centers: Hyundai has established R&D centers in India to focus on vehicle design, engineering, and technology development.
  • Sales & Service Network: Hyundai has an extensive sales and service network across India, ensuring customer satisfaction and support.

Application Process

  • Online Application:
    • Candidates can apply for job openings through Hyundai's official careers portal. The application process typically involves submitting a resume, filling out an online form, and providing relevant documents.
  • Screening and Shortlisting:
    • After the application submission, the HR team screens the applications based on qualifications, experience, and suitability for the role. Shortlisted candidates are then contacted for further assessments.
  • Interviews:
    • The interview process may include multiple rounds, such as technical interviews, HR interviews, and panel discussions. Candidates may be assessed on their skills, knowledge, and cultural fit with the organization.
  • Offer and Onboarding:
    • Successful candidates receive an offer letter outlining the terms of employment. Upon acceptance, the onboarding process begins, which includes orientation sessions and necessary documentation.
